Case studies

London Restaurant Expansion & Fit-Out
A fast-growing, London-based restaurant group approached Vercas Financial to secure capital for their highly anticipated next site. Recognising the critical nature of this growth phase, we engaged our extensive network of lenders to build a bespoke funding strategy. By structuring a tailored package combining Asset Finance and Commercial Loans, we ensured the business had rapid access to the exact capital required to bring their new culinary vision to life.

The Challenge: Securing multifaceted funding for a pivotal new restaurant site during a rapid expansion phase.

The Solution: A customised, hybrid funding facility utilising both Asset Finance and Commercial Loans.

The Outcome: Seamless capital deployment, enabling an on-time fit-out and continued business growth.

Fleet Acquisition for Private Hire Firm
When an established private hire company needed to scale its operations to meet growing consumer demand, Vercas Financial stepped in to facilitate the acquisition of five new vehicles. We streamlined the asset finance process, providing a competitive solution that allowed the client to upgrade their fleet, elevate their service capacity, and keep their business moving forward without disrupting day-to-day cash flow.

The Challenge: Expanding service capacity quickly without tying up essential working capital.

The Solution: Fast-tracked, competitive asset finance for five new commercial vehicles.

The Outcome: An immediate fleet upgrade and increased revenue potential for the client.

Strategic Gym Equipment Partnership
Vercas Financial has formed a strategic partnership with a premier provider of professional-grade gym equipment. Through this ongoing collaboration, we act as an integrated finance partner, offering innovative and flexible funding solutions directly to their client base. This ensures that fitness entrepreneurs and commercial gym owners can seamlessly acquire top-tier equipment whilst preserving their operational liquidity.

The Challenge: Providing end-buyers with accessible, frictionless ways to fund premium gym equipment.

The Solution: A dedicated introducer partnership offering integrated financial solutions to clients.

The Outcome: Enhanced buying power for gym owners and streamlined equipment acquisition.
